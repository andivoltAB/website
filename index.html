About Andivolt AB

Andivolt AB is a Swedish limited liability company (aktiebolag) based in Gothenburg. We work with printed circuit board and antenna technology: schematic capture, high-speed and RF layout, antenna design and tuning, prototype builds and small-series fabrication.

Our customers are product companies that need wireless hardware to work in the real world — compact boards, clean signal integrity and antennas that keep their performance once they are inside the enclosure.

What we do

PCB design

Schematic capture, multilayer stack-up, controlled impedance routing, power integrity and design-for-manufacture reviews.

Antenna design

PCB, chip and custom antennas for GNSS, Wi-Fi, BLE, LTE and NB-IoT, including matching networks and in-enclosure tuning.

Prototyping & fabrication

Bare-board fabrication, assembly and small-series production through a qualified supplier network, with full documentation packages.

Test & verification

Return loss and efficiency measurements, radiated performance checks and support through pre-compliance and certification testing.
